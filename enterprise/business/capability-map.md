# Business Capability Map

## Level 1 capabilities

### Institutional Governance
Board/general assembly support, committees, policies, procedures, meetings, decisions, delegations, authorities and governance evidence.

### Strategy & Performance
Strategic planning, objectives, initiatives, KPIs, performance reviews and institutional reporting.

### Risk, Compliance & Quality
Risk management, obligations, controls, compliance assessments, findings, corrective actions, quality management and continuous improvement.

### People & Workforce
People records, HR, organizational assignments, workforce lifecycle, training and capability development.

### Membership & Stakeholder Services
Memberships, beneficiaries, volunteers, donors, partners, CRM and stakeholder engagement.

### Programs, Projects & Impact
Programs, projects, initiatives, activities, beneficiaries, outputs, outcomes, impact measurement and lessons learned.

### Financial & Resource Management
Finance, budgets, grants/donations, procurement interfaces, assets, inventory, contracts and resource stewardship.

### Knowledge, Documents & Records
Document control, records, institutional knowledge, templates, classifications and retention.

### Communications & Engagement
Events, media, campaigns, public relations and notifications.

### Digital Platform Operations
Identity/access, workflow, configuration, integrations, master data, reporting/analytics, audit, platform operations and later AI-enabled capabilities.

## Capability modeling rule
Capabilities describe what the institution must be able to do; they do not prescribe screens, databases, departments or implementation technologies. Detailed processes and systems are mapped only after capability boundaries are understood.
