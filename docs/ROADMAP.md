# MSALAWI Product Roadmap

The roadmap is capability-driven and may evolve through ADR/product decisions. Version numbers communicate product maturity, not a promise to implement every aspirational domain immediately.

## v0.1.0-alpha — Baseline
Repository/solution skeleton, .NET API baseline, React shell, health endpoint, initial Person slice, in-memory persistence, tests, Docker Compose and CI baseline.

## v0.2.0 — Institutional Foundation
Enterprise knowledge base, reference architecture, bounded contexts, development standards, PostgreSQL persistence foundation, tenant context/isolation foundation, audit foundation and core organization/identity contracts.

## v0.3.x — Identity, People & Organization
Production-oriented authentication integration, authorization policies, Person/User separation, organizational structures, positions/assignments and tenant administration.

## v0.4.x — Governance Foundation
Governed instruments, meetings, decisions, delegations, authority rules and auditable governance lifecycles.

## v0.5.x — Workflow Foundation
Versioned workflow definitions, instances, work items, approvals, assignment resolution, deadlines/escalations and business-context integration patterns.

## v0.6.x — Documents, Compliance & Reporting Foundations
Document/record controls, compliance/risk primitives and reporting/read-model foundations.

## v0.7–0.9 — Prioritized Business Domains
Introduce selected nonprofit business domains based on validated product priorities (for example membership, volunteering, programs/projects and stakeholder services), consuming the platform engines rather than duplicating them.

## v1.0 — Production Readiness
Security hardening, observability, backup/recovery, performance/scalability validation, operational runbooks, deployment environments, accessibility/bilingual verification, data governance, migration strategy and release/support readiness.

## Roadmap gate
No business domain enters implementation solely because it appears on this roadmap. Product priority, business model, regulatory context, domain model and acceptance criteria are required first.
